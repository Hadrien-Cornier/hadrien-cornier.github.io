 Deep Reinforcement Learning 
 Lectures: 9 - 10 am on Wednesdays and 8 - 10 am on Fridays, both in Hearst Annex A1 
 The lectures will be streamed and recorded . The course is not being offered as an online course, and the videos are provided only for your personal informational and entertainment purposes. They are not part of any course requirement or degree-bearing university program.

 IMPORTANT : If you are an undergraduate or 5th year MS student, or a non-EECS graduate student, please fill out this form to apply for enrollment into the Fall 2023 version of the course. Do not email the course instructors about enrollment -- all students who fill out the form will be reviewed. We will enroll off of this form during the first week of class. We will not be using the official CalCentral wait list, just this form.

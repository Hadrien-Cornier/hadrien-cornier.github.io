What this shows +

One arm, one speed, one scene. The learned methods are represented by simple rules. These runs illustrate mechanisms.

Families overlap. Action chunks can come from a diffusion model or a generalist policy. The synchronous example waits between chunks; not every chunked policy does.

The other sketches are set to the red-cube task. The generalist sketch also reads the red/blue instruction. Real systems can combine all of these abilities.

Real-time chunks keep the commands that are already due to run. The blue segment shows those committed commands while the rest of the plan can change.

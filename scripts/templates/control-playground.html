What this shows +

One arm, one speed, one scene. The learned methods are represented by simple rules. These runs illustrate mechanisms and failure causes.

Diffusion describes how actions are generated. Pretraining describes what the model learned before this task. π₀ combines a pretrained vision-language model with a flow-based action expert. These ideas can be combined.

Execution timing is a separate choice. The RTC paper overlaps computation with motion for diffusion and flow policies. This toy also applies that timing idea to the action-transformer sketch.

The blue path marks commands that are already committed. New observations still take time to change movement.

Challenges introduce specific toy errors: a moving target, a sudden blocker, swapped visual labels, or wrong camera coordinates. Grasping, collisions, and the real cube identity decide the result.

The action-transformer, diffusion, and world-model sketches are set to the red-cube task. The VLA sketch also reads the red/blue instruction.

Papers and their limits: ACT, real-time chunking, Diffusion Policy, π₀, V-JEPA 2-AC.
